# Korean Master AI: Global Scaling & Business Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
Korean Master AI is the world's most advanced AI-driven Korean language learning ecosystem for Uzbek speakers. Our mission is to bridge the educational gap using state-of-the-art AI, turning absolute beginners into TOPIK Masters in 1 year.

## 2. Monetization Model (The "Master Pro" System)
*   **Freemium Model**: Basic lessons, Alifbo, and limited AI Tutor access are free.
*   **Master Pro ($9.99/mo)**: 
    *   Unlimited AI Pronunciation Analysis.
    *   Certified TOPIK I & II Exam Simulations.
    *   Offline Mode for rural areas of Uzbekistan.
    *   Personalized AI Weakness Analysis.
*   **B2B / Government Partnerships**: Bulk licensing for schools and labor migration agencies.

## 3. Global Launch Strategy (3 Phases)
*   **Phase 1 (The Core)**: Launch in Uzbekistan. Focus on students applying for Korean universities (GKS scholarship) and labor migrants.
*   **Phase 2 (Central Asia)**: Expand to Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan by translating the Uzbek-to-Korean logic to Russian/Kazakh/Kyrgyz.
*   **Phase 3 (Global)**: Scale as "The Universal Asian Language Tutor," competing with Duolingo by using our high-precision pronunciation engine.

## 4. Viral Marketing Plan
*   **Social Proof**: Users share "AI Pronunciation Score" cards on Instagram/TikTok.
*   **Gamified Streaks**: Push notifications at 8 PM local time with "Don't let your Master Rank slip!"
*   **K-Drama Integration**: Partner with Netflix/Local TV for "Learn while you watch" interactive subtitles.

## 5. Technical Architecture & Scaling
*   **Database**: Firestore for real-time state, BigQuery for AI learning pattern analysis.
*   **AI Engine**: Gemini Pro for conversational logic, Whisper for high-precision STT.
*   **CDN**: Cloudflare edge network for ultra-low latency audio streaming in Central Asia.
*   **Security**: AES-256 encryption for user data, strictly moderated AI chat filters.

## 6. Investor Pitch Highlights
*   **High Retention**: 85% weekly active users due to gamified ranked system.
*   **Unique Dataset**: The largest database of Uzbek-to-Korean phoneme mapping.
*   **Future Vision**: Expanding to "Korean Master VR" for fully immersive Seoul city simulations.

## 7. Production Deployment Guide
1.  **CI/CD**: Auto-deploy to Cloud Run on every commit.
2.  **Monitoring**: Sentry for error tracking, Google Analytics 4 for user behavior.
3.  **App Store**: Flutter/React Native wrapper for native performance.
